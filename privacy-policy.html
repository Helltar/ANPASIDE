Privacy Policy

ANPASIDE · last updated 23 July 2026

ANPASIDE is an offline development environment for the MIDletPascal language: you write, compile and run J2ME programs entirely on your own device. This policy explains what the app does — and does not — do with your data.

Information we collect

None. ANPASIDE does not collect, transmit or sell any personally identifiable information such as your name, e-mail address, location or contacts. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

How your data is stored

Your projects — source files, resources, build settings and compiled output — are stored only in the app's own storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded to the developer or to any third party. Removing your projects, or uninstalling the app, deletes this data.

Sharing and export

When you explicitly choose to share a file or export a project, ANPASIDE hands that file to another app you pick from the Android share sheet. This only happens when you initiate it, and where the file goes afterwards is governed by the app you selected, not by ANPASIDE.

Permissions

ANPASIDE requests a single permission, INTERNET, so that a program you build and run in the built-in J2ME emulator can access the network if its own code was written to do so. ANPASIDE itself does not use this permission to gather or send your data — you are in control of the code you build.

Children's privacy

ANPASIDE does not collect personally identifiable information from children or from anyone else.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact
